Common questions

What size is an Instagram Story photo?

A full-screen Instagram Story uses a vertical 9:16 canvas. 1080 × 1920 pixels is the standard working size used by our resizer.

Will my photo be uploaded?

No. The InstaTimestamp resizer processes the selected image locally in your browser.

Can I resize a landscape photo for Stories?

Yes. Choose Crop to fill, Fit to keep the whole image, or Blur fill for a vertical background.
