Common questions

Instagram last post checker FAQ

How do I see when someone last posted on Instagram? +
Open their public profile, open the newest post or Reel, copy the link, and paste it into the checker above. The tool shows the exact posting date and time.
Can I check by username? +
Not with this free timestamp decoder. You need the latest public post or Reel URL. The tool does not automatically fetch a profile's newest post from a username.
Does it work with Instagram Reels? +
Yes. Standard Instagram post and Reel links are supported.
Can I check a private account? +
Only if you can already access the private post and copy its link. The checker itself does not bypass Instagram privacy controls or fetch private profile content.
Does the account owner get notified? +
No notification is generated by decoding a post URL locally in your browser.
Is the timestamp estimated? +
No. The timestamp is decoded from the Instagram post identifier rather than guessed from a relative label such as “3 days ago.”
