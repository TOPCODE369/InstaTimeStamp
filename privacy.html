Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

Welcome to InstaTimestamp ("we," "our," or "us"). This Privacy Policy describes how information is handled when you visit instatimestamp.com or use our tools. Different tools handle data in different ways, as explained below.

1. Information and tool inputs

  • Timestamp checker: When you paste an Instagram post or Reel link into the timestamp checker, the timestamp is decoded in your browser. The link is not sent to our application server for that calculation.
  • Image resizers: Images selected for our browser-based resizing tools are processed locally on your device rather than uploaded to our application server for resizing.
  • Competitor posting-time analyzer: Posting-time calculations from post or Reel links supplied to the free analyzer run in your browser. The analyzer does not automatically fetch an Instagram account's posts.
  • Instagram Story Viewer: If you enter a public Instagram username, the username is sent to our Cloudflare Worker and a third-party Story data provider (HikerAPI) to request publicly available Stories. Responses may be cached for a short period to operate and protect the service. The Story Viewer is not a browser-only tool.
  • Messages you send us: If you email us, we receive your email address and the details you choose to share so we can respond.
  • Technical information: Our hosting, security, analytics, and advertising providers may process information such as IP address, browser and device details, approximate location, pages requested, and request timestamps to operate and protect the website.

2. Why we use information

We use information as needed to provide our tools, respond to support requests, understand aggregate website usage, maintain security, prevent abuse, and, where enabled, support advertising. We do not sell the Instagram links submitted to our browser-based timestamp checker.

3. Cloudflare Web Analytics and website infrastructure

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to understand website traffic, such as page visits, referring websites, broad geographic information, and browser or device information. Cloudflare also provides infrastructure and security services. Cloudflare processes technical data under its own privacy practices. Learn more in Cloudflare's Privacy Policy.

4. Google AdSense, cookies, and advertising

Our website includes Google AdSense integration. Ads may appear when the site is approved and advertising is enabled. Google and other participating third-party advertising vendors may use cookies, web beacons, IP addresses, or similar identifiers to serve and measure ads.

Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a person's previous visits to this website or other websites. Google's advertising cookies enable Google and its partners to show ads based on visits to this and other websites. Other approved advertising vendors or networks may also use their own cookies or similar technologies when ads are served.

You can control or opt out of personalized advertising through Google's My Ad Center. You can also visit AboutAds for choices regarding some other participating advertising companies. Opting out of personalization does not necessarily prevent all ads or cookies.

For more information about Google's data practices, read How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Depending on where you live, you may be shown a consent or privacy-choices message when advertising technologies are used. You may also control or block cookies through your browser settings; doing so may affect some features or advertising.

5. Other third-party services

  • Cloudflare: Hosting, website security, Worker services, and privacy-focused Web Analytics.
  • HikerAPI: Third-party data provider used by the Instagram Story Viewer to retrieve available public Stories for the username submitted.
  • Google AdSense: Advertising integration and, if approved and enabled, ad delivery and measurement.
  • Google Fonts: Some pages load fonts from Google's servers, which may receive technical request information.

The payment/checkout product shown as a Pro preview is not currently available for purchase. We do not currently collect payment card information through that preview. This policy will be updated if payment processing or other services are introduced.

6. Data retention and security

Browser-based decoding does not require us to store pasted post links or resized images on our application server. Public Story Viewer requests may be held briefly in a cache; infrastructure and third-party providers may also retain technical logs according to their operational practices. We keep email correspondence only as needed for support and legitimate business purposes, and take reasonable measures to protect information. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

7. Your privacy choices

You may use browser settings to manage cookies, control personalized advertising using the links above, or contact us to ask about information you have provided directly to us. Depending on your location, applicable privacy laws may provide additional rights to access, correct, delete, or object to certain processing. To make a request, contact us using the email address below.

8. Children's privacy

InstaTimestamp is not designed for children under 13. We do not knowingly solicit personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as our features, third-party services, or legal obligations change. The date at the top of the page will show when the policy was last revised.
