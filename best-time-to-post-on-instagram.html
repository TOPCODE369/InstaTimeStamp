What the research actually says

Why do different Instagram timing studies disagree?

Because they examine different sets of accounts, time periods and engagement measures. There is no single universally winning hour.

Buffer: 9.6 million Instagram posts

Published September 23, 2026. Studied posts from January 2024 through December 2025, using median engagement rate. Wednesday ranked strongest overall, with Thursday morning and Wednesday midday/evening among the leading hours. Friday and Saturday were weaker in this dataset. Read Buffer's methodology →

Sprout Social: a different engagement lens

Published March 31, 2026. Its cross-platform study covered nearly two billion engagements across 307,000 global social profiles. For Instagram, Sprout reported strong windows on Mondays (2–4 PM), Tuesdays (1–7 PM), Wednesdays (noon–9 PM) and Thursdays (noon–2 PM). Those broad ranges differ from Buffer's top single slots. Read Sprout's research →

Our editorial interpretation: Try a few consistent windows, then compare results from posts of similar formats and topics. Don't average the two studies into a fabricated “scientific” score, and don't assume a high-ranking benchmark is your own audience's peak.
Reels, feed posts and Stories

What is the best time to post Instagram Reels?

For Reels, a reasonable starting experiment is a midweek evening slot, such as Wednesday 6 PM in your audience's timezone. But Buffer's 9.6-million-post study grouped Instagram formats together: it did not establish a standalone Reels-only winner. Don't present an all-format average as a proven Reel-specific statistic.

Reels

Test one midweek evening slot against one morning or midday slot. Watch Reel-specific outcomes such as reach, watch time and shares, not only likes.

Feed posts and carousels

Use the table's suggested times as a baseline. Compare similar post formats over several weeks so content differences don't overwhelm your timing test.

Stories

For time-limited Stories, consistency and your audience's viewing habits may matter more than finding a single perfect publishing minute.

India, US and UK

Use your audience timezone, not necessarily your home timezone. A creator in India who targets New York viewers can plan around New York local hours using the planner.

A practical strategy

How to find the best posting time for your account

Industry benchmarks help you choose initial hours. Your own audience, niche and content performance help you choose what to keep.

Choose three time windows

Try a research-backed time from the table, a second plausible time, and a control slot. Use the audience timezone consistently.

Post comparable content

Test over several weeks. Compare Reels with Reels, carousels with carousels, and avoid judging a time slot by a single viral post.

Measure outcomes

Use Instagram Insights to check reach, shares, saves or clicks as appropriate. Keep the hours that perform best with your real audience.

Frequently asked questions

Instagram posting time FAQs

What is the best time to post on Instagram today?

It depends on the day and where your audience lives. The table above gives Buffer's best single hour for each weekday, and the timezone planner finds your next upcoming research-backed slot. Your Instagram Insights are the better guide once you have enough data.

What is the best day to post on Instagram in 2026?

Wednesday was the strongest day in Buffer's September 2026 Instagram analysis, followed by Thursday and Tuesday. Sprout Social's 2026 analysis also found strong midweek engagement, but results differ by dataset.

What is the best time to post on Instagram in India?

If most of your audience is in India, begin by testing the local IST times in the day-by-day table, such as Wednesday noon or Thursday 9 AM. Do not convert those baseline hours from US Eastern time; Buffer's research already normalized them to the audience's local clock.

What is the best time to post in the US or UK?

Start with the same audience-local time recommendations, then choose the correct audience timezone in the planner. US timezones vary, and New York's daylight-saving rules differ from London's. What matters is where your viewers are, not where you live.

What is the best time to post Reels on Instagram?

Midweek evenings are a useful starting experiment based on the all-format Instagram research, but there is no universal Reels-only winning hour established by the Buffer dataset described here. Compare your own Reel reach, shares and watch time across multiple time windows.

Can I see my competitors' best-performing posting times?

InstaTimestamp can show the posting times and weekday pattern of up to 10 public post/Reel links you provide for one competitor. It does not retrieve engagement statistics or determine which of those posts performed best. Posting time and engagement performance are different metrics.

Does this planner connect to Instagram or collect my data?

No. The timezone planner uses built-in browser date and timezone functions. The timing recommendations are from cited third-party studies, and no Instagram account access is needed.
