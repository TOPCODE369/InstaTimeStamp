Frequently asked questions

Can I enter a username and automatically analyze every post?

Not in the free, browser-only version. An Instagram username doesn't contain a history of post links. Automatic discovery requires a separate retrieval system. This tool deliberately avoids paid lookup requests.

Does this show the best time to post on Instagram?

No. It shows when the supplied posts were published, not which times caused the most engagement. It cannot identify the highest-performing time slots without engagement data.

How many posts can I analyze for free?

The free version analyzes up to 10 valid unique post or Reel links for one competitor. The planned Pro version will support larger samples and side-by-side competitor comparison.

Do you store or fetch my competitor's posts?

No. This tool decodes the links in your browser, without requesting the posts' media or storing your input on an InstaTimestamp server.

What time does an Instagram post's shortcode represent?

InstaTimestamp decodes the timestamp encoded into the media ID. It is a practical indicator of the media's creation/publication time, not a measurement of when followers first saw it or when it reached peak engagement.
